New Employee Onboarding Checklist

Purpose

This checklist walks new employees and their managers through the first 90 days at Contoso, from pre-start preparation through full ramp-up. It's intended to be used alongside, not instead of, the detailed guides it references for IT setup and benefits enrollment.

Before day one

  • HR sends a welcome email with start date, reporting manager, and first-day logistics (remote link or office address).
  • IT provisions a laptop and standard accounts ahead of the start date; see the New Hire IT Equipment and Software Setup guide for what's included.
  • Manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the team.
  • New hire completes and returns any outstanding I-9 or tax documentation requested by HR.

Day one

  • Attend the New Hire Orientation session (company overview, Code of Conduct walkthrough, benefits introduction).
  • Receive laptop and equipment; complete initial account activation (email, single sign-on).
  • Install and configure the VPN client following the VPN Setup Guide — required before accessing any internal systems remotely.
  • Meet with manager to review role expectations and the 30/60/90-day plan.

Week one

  • Complete mandatory compliance training: security awareness, harassment prevention, and data handling (see the Acceptable Use and Security Policy).
  • Set up direct deposit and review payroll details in the HR portal.
  • Begin benefits enrollment — see the Employee Benefits Overview. Enrollment must be completed within 30 days of the start date or coverage is delayed until the next open enrollment period.
  • Shadow team meetings and review team documentation with onboarding buddy.

First 30 days

  • Complete benefits enrollment deadline (30 days from start date).
  • Meet individually with key cross-functional partners identified by manager.
  • Begin taking on scoped project work per the 30/60/90-day plan.
  • Confirm PTO accrual has started correctly by checking the HR portal (see the PTO Policy for accrual timing).

First 90 days

  • Complete the introductory period; remote work eligibility begins for applicable roles per the Remote Work Policy.
  • Manager completes a 90-day check-in covering ramp progress and any support needed.
  • New hire provides feedback on the onboarding experience via the HR portal survey.

Manager responsibilities

Managers are responsible for ensuring the new hire's equipment and accounts are ready before day one, assigning an onboarding buddy, scheduling the 30/60/90-day check-ins, and confirming compliance training is completed on time. Managers who need to escalate a delayed equipment order should contact IT through the Help Desk Ticketing and Support Guide.

Questions

New hires with questions about any step in this checklist should reach out to their onboarding buddy first, then their manager or HR business partner for anything unresolved.
