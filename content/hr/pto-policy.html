Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Purpose

Contoso provides paid time off to support employees' rest, personal needs, and overall well-being. This policy explains how PTO accrues, how to request time off, how unused time carries over between years, and how PTO is handled at termination.

Accrual rates

PTO accrues each pay period based on years of service and employment type. Accrual begins on an employee's start date, though newly accrued PTO is not available for use until after the 90-day introductory period, except in jurisdictions where law requires earlier availability.

Years of serviceAnnual accrual (full-time)Annual accrual (part-time, pro-rated)
0-2 years15 days7.5 days
3-5 years20 days10 days
6-9 years23 days11.5 days
10+ years25 days12.5 days

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to their scheduled hours. Contractors and interns are not eligible for PTO accrual under this policy.

Requesting time off

Submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least five business days in advance for planned absences of one day or more. Same-day or emergency requests should be communicated directly to your manager as soon as possible and logged in the system retroactively. Managers approve or deny requests based on team coverage; requests should not be assumed approved until confirmed in writing.

During company-designated blackout periods (typically the last two weeks of each fiscal quarter), PTO requests longer than two consecutive days require additional approval from a director-level manager.

Company holidays

In addition to accrued PTO, Contoso observes the following paid holidays each year: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the following Friday, and Christmas Day. Employees in regions with different statutory holidays should refer to their local HR addendum for the applicable holiday schedule.

Sick leave vs. PTO

Contoso maintains a separate sick leave bank distinct from PTO, accruing at one day per month up to a maximum of 10 days per year. Sick leave should be used for illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member. Employees do not need to specify a reason when requesting standard PTO, but sick leave usage is tracked separately for compliance with state and local sick leave laws.

Carryover and payout

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. PTO accrued beyond the carryover cap is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out.

Upon voluntary or involuntary termination, employees are paid out for any accrued and unused PTO in their final paycheck, in accordance with state law. Unused sick leave is not paid out upon termination.

Extended leave

For extended absences due to a serious health condition, the birth or adoption of a child, or care for an immediate family member, employees should contact HR to discuss eligibility for leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) or applicable state leave programs. Extended leave is administered separately from standard PTO and does not draw down an employee's PTO balance unless the employee elects to use PTO to supplement unpaid leave.

Questions

Questions about PTO balances, accrual, or requests that were not resolved by a manager should be directed to your HR business partner or submitted through the HR portal's help form.
