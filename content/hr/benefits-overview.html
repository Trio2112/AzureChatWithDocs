Employee Benefits Overview

Purpose

This document summarizes the benefits available to full-time Contoso employees. It is intended as a plain-language overview; full plan documents and legal summary plan descriptions are available through the benefits portal.

Eligibility and enrollment

Full-time employees (30+ scheduled hours per week) are eligible for benefits starting the first of the month following their start date. New hires must complete enrollment within 30 days of their start date, as outlined in the New Employee Onboarding Checklist, or coverage is delayed until the next open enrollment period in November.

Health insurance

Contoso offers three medical plan tiers through Northwind Health, along with dental and vision coverage. Employees can add a spouse, domestic partner, and/or dependents during enrollment.

Retirement savings

Contoso offers a 401(k) plan through Fabrikam Retirement Services. Contoso matches 100% of the first 3% of salary an employee contributes, and 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum match of 4% of salary. Matching contributions vest immediately. Employees are automatically enrolled at a 3% contribution rate 60 days after their start date unless they opt out or change their contribution rate sooner.

Wellness stipend

Employees may claim up to $50 per month toward gym memberships, fitness classes, or wellness apps through the expense reimbursement system, using expense category "Wellness Stipend." Unused monthly amounts do not carry over.

Parental leave

Contoso provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child, available to any parent regardless of gender, in addition to any leave available under FMLA or applicable state programs. Parental leave should be coordinated with HR at least 30 days before the expected start date when possible. See the PTO Policy for how parental leave interacts with PTO accrual.

Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

All employees and their household members have access to free, confidential counseling and support services through the EAP, including up to six counseling sessions per issue per year, financial planning consultations, and legal referral services. The EAP is available 24/7 by phone and is not tied to medical plan enrollment.

Open enrollment

Outside of qualifying life events (marriage, birth of a child, loss of other coverage, etc.), benefits elections can only be changed during the annual open enrollment period in November, with changes taking effect January 1st. Qualifying life events must be reported within 30 days of the event to make off-cycle changes.
