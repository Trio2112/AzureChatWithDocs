Code of Conduct

Purpose

This Code of Conduct describes the standards of behavior Contoso expects from all employees, contractors, and officers. It applies regardless of work location, including remote work arrangements, and regardless of whether interactions happen in person, over email, or through internal chat tools.

Core values

Contoso employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues and customers with respect, and make decisions that consider the long-term interests of the company, its customers, and its employees over short-term convenience.

Respectful workplace and anti-harassment

Contoso maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or any other characteristic protected by law. This applies equally to in-person, remote, and virtual interactions, including video calls and chat messages.

Employees who experience or witness harassment or discrimination should report it to their manager, HR business partner, or through the anonymous ethics hotline described below. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this policy and grounds for disciplinary action.

Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role at Contoso — for example, a financial stake in a vendor or competitor, or a direct reporting relationship with a close family member. Disclosures should be made to your manager and HR as soon as the conflict becomes apparent, and in all cases before participating in any related business decision.

Confidentiality and data handling

Employees regularly have access to confidential information, including customer data, financial results before public disclosure, and proprietary source code. This information must not be shared outside Contoso, or with colleagues who do not have a legitimate business need to know, regardless of work location.

Confidential information must only be accessed through approved, secured channels (including the corporate VPN when working remotely) and must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts. Detailed technical requirements for handling confidential data are covered in the Acceptable Use and Security Policy, which employees are required to review annually.

Use of company property

Company equipment, accounts, and facilities are provided for business use. Reasonable personal use of company equipment (for example, checking personal email on a lunch break) is permitted, but employees should have no expectation of privacy on company-owned systems, which may be monitored consistent with the Acceptable Use and Security Policy.

Gifts and business courtesies

Employees may accept or offer modest business courtesies (for example, a meal during a business meeting) but must not accept or offer gifts, payments, or favors intended to influence a business decision. Gifts valued over $100 must be disclosed to your manager, and cash or cash-equivalent gifts of any value must always be declined.

Reporting violations

Suspected violations of this Code of Conduct can be reported to your manager, your HR business partner, or anonymously through the ethics hotline at ethics.contoso.internal, which is operated by an independent third party. Reports are investigated promptly and confidentially to the extent possible.

Disciplinary process

Violations of this Code of Conduct are addressed through Contoso's standard disciplinary process, which may range from a documented verbal warning to immediate termination depending on the severity of the violation. HR will determine the appropriate response in consultation with the employee's management chain and, where applicable, Legal.
