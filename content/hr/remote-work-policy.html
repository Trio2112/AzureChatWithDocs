Remote Work Policy

Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period are eligible to work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. Contractors and interns are not eligible for remote work under this policy unless an exception is granted in writing by HR.

Equipment

Contoso provides a standard laptop, headset, and monitor for remote employees. Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable home internet connection of at least 25 Mbps. Requests for additional equipment should be submitted through the IT asset request form.

Security requirements

Remote employees must connect to internal systems through the company VPN whenever accessing confidential data, source code, or HR records. Split-tunneling is disabled by default. See the IT knowledge base for VPN client setup instructions. Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN enabled is a policy violation.

Core hours

Remote employees are expected to be reachable between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone, and to attend scheduled team meetings with camera on unless otherwise agreed with their manager.
