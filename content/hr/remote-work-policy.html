Remote Work Policy

Purpose

Contoso supports flexible work arrangements that allow employees to perform their duties outside of a traditional office setting while maintaining productivity, collaboration, and the security of company information. This policy defines who is eligible to work remotely, how to request a remote work arrangement, and the expectations placed on employees who work outside the office.

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Contoso. It does not apply to roles that, by their nature, require an on-site presence (for example, facilities, warehouse, and front-desk roles), which are addressed separately in each department's operating procedures.

Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period are eligible to work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. Employees who have not yet completed their introductory period may request an exception through their manager and HR business partner, but exceptions are granted sparingly and generally require a documented business reason.

Contractors and interns are not eligible for remote work under this policy unless an exception is granted in writing by HR and reflected in the contractor's statement of work. Eligibility is reviewed annually as part of the performance review cycle, and may be revoked if performance or engagement concerns arise while working remotely.

Requesting a remote work arrangement

To request a remote work arrangement, submit a Remote Work Request through the HR portal at least two weeks before the desired start date. Your manager will review the request based on team coverage needs, the nature of your role, and your recent performance history. Approved arrangements are documented in Workday and reviewed every six months.

Employees relocating to a different state or country must notify HR before doing so, even if their role is fully remote. Tax, payroll, and benefits eligibility can change significantly based on work location, and unapproved relocations may result in a delay or interruption of payroll processing.

Equipment and reimbursement

Contoso provides a standard laptop, headset, and monitor for remote employees. New hires and employees transitioning to a remote arrangement should reference the IT equipment setup guide for the ordering and provisioning process. Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable home internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload.

Remote employees may submit reasonable expenses for a desk chair or ergonomic accessories, up to $250 per calendar year, through the standard expense reimbursement process. Requests for additional equipment beyond the standard kit should be submitted through the IT asset request form and require manager approval.

Security requirements

Remote employees must connect to internal systems through the company VPN whenever accessing confidential data, source code, customer records, or HR records. Split-tunneling is disabled by default, meaning all traffic is routed through the corporate network while connected. See the IT knowledge base's VPN setup guide for client installation instructions.

Working from public Wi-Fi (coffee shops, airports, hotels) without the VPN enabled is a policy violation and may result in disciplinary action. Employees must lock their screen whenever stepping away from their device and must not allow family members or others to use company-issued equipment. Additional detail on acceptable use and data handling is covered in the Acceptable Use and Security Policy and the Code of Conduct.

Core hours and availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their assigned local time zone, and to attend scheduled team meetings with camera on unless otherwise agreed with their manager. Outside of core hours, employees may structure their schedule flexibly to accommodate personal commitments, provided they meet their weekly hour commitments and deadlines.

Employees working remotely from a different time zone than their team should coordinate a mutually agreeable overlap window with their manager and document it in their team's shared calendar.

Performance expectations

Remote work is a privilege extended based on trust and demonstrated performance. Managers evaluate remote employees using the same performance criteria as in-office employees: quality of work, responsiveness, and collaboration. There is no expectation that remote employees be "always on" or respond to messages outside of their normal working hours.

Policy violations

Violations of this policy, including repeated failure to use the VPN when required, missing core-hours availability without notice, or misuse of company equipment, may result in the remote work arrangement being revoked and, in serious or repeated cases, further disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Policy review

This policy is reviewed annually by HR and People Operations leadership. Employees will be notified of material changes at least 30 days before they take effect. Questions about this policy should be directed to your HR business partner.
