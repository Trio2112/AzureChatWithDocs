Help Desk Ticketing and Support Guide

Overview

This guide explains how to get IT support at Contoso, how tickets are prioritized, and what response times to expect. Many common issues can be resolved faster through self-service than by opening a ticket — check the relevant guide first.

Before opening a ticket

For the most common issues, self-service resources resolve the problem faster than waiting for a ticket response:

  • Forgotten password or MFA issues — see the Password Reset and Account Security Guide.
  • VPN connection problems — see the troubleshooting section of the VPN Setup Guide.
  • New equipment or software requests — use the IT asset request form referenced in the New Hire IT Equipment and Software Setup guide.
  • General outages — check the IT status page at status.contoso.internal before opening a ticket, since widespread issues are already being tracked there.

Submitting a ticket

Open a ticket at helpdesk.contoso.internal or by emailing support@contoso.internal, which automatically creates a ticket. Include your device name (found in Settings > About), a description of the issue, and any error codes or screenshots. Tickets with more detail are resolved faster because they avoid a back-and-forth clarification round.

Severity levels and response times

SeverityDefinitionExamplesFirst response SLA
Severity 1 (Critical) Security incident or complete inability to work Suspected account compromise, lost/stolen laptop, production outage 30 minutes, 24/7
Severity 2 (High) Major function impaired, no workaround Cannot access email, VPN completely down for multiple users 2 business hours
Severity 3 (Normal) Standard issue with a workaround available Individual VPN connectivity issue, single application error 1 business day
Severity 4 (Low) Requests and minor issues Software installation request, peripheral request 3 business days

Severity is initially set by the requester when submitting the ticket and may be adjusted by the help desk team after triage. If you believe your ticket's severity was set incorrectly, reply in the ticket thread rather than opening a duplicate ticket.

Escalation

If a ticket has not received a response within its SLA window, reply to the ticket and cc your manager. For Severity 1 issues specifically, you may also call the 24/7 IT emergency line listed on the IT status page in addition to opening a ticket — do not rely on the emergency line alone, since a ticket is still needed for tracking and follow-up.

After-hours support

Standard help desk hours are Monday-Friday, 7:00 AM-7:00 PM local time. Outside these hours, only Severity 1 issues (security incidents, complete outages) are actively staffed; Severity 2-4 tickets submitted after hours are picked up at the start of the next business day.

New hire support

Tickets related to new-hire equipment or account setup delays should be flagged with the "new hire" tag when submitted, which routes them to an expedited queue given their time sensitivity relative to a new employee's start date. See the New Hire IT Equipment and Software Setup guide for what's normally provisioned before day one.
