Password Reset and Account Security Guide

Overview

This guide covers Contoso's password requirements, how to reset a forgotten password, how to set up and manage multi-factor authentication (MFA), and what to do if you suspect your account has been compromised.

Password requirements

Corporate account passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Passwords may not reuse any of your last 10 passwords and expire every 180 days. We recommend using a passphrase (a short sentence with numbers/symbols substituted in) rather than a single complex word, as passphrases are both easier to remember and harder to crack.

Resetting your password

If you remember your current password

Go to account.contoso.internal, sign in normally, and select Security > Change Password. You'll need to enter your current password once and your new password twice.

If you've forgotten your password

From the sign-in page, select "Forgot password?" and follow the prompts. You will receive a verification code via the Microsoft Authenticator app (or SMS to your registered phone number if Authenticator isn't available). Enter the code, then set a new password meeting the requirements above. Self-service reset is available 24/7 and does not require contacting the help desk.

If self-service reset fails repeatedly, contact the IT help desk following the process in the Help Desk Ticketing and Support Guide. For security reasons, the help desk will verify your identity via your manager or a government-issued ID before manually resetting your password.

Setting up multi-factor authentication

All Contoso accounts require MFA. New hires are prompted to set up MFA during initial account activation on their first day (see the New Hire IT Equipment and Software Setup guide). To set up or change your MFA method, go to account.contoso.internal and select Security > Multi-Factor Authentication. We recommend the Microsoft Authenticator app over SMS, since SMS-based codes are more vulnerable to interception.

MFA is required both for standard sign-in and for connecting to the VPN — see the VPN Setup Guide for how MFA is used during VPN connection specifically. If you get a new phone, re-register Authenticator before your old phone is wiped or deactivated to avoid being locked out.

Account lockout policy

After 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes as a brute-force protection measure. Repeated lockouts within a short period may trigger a temporary hold requiring help desk verification before the account is reopened.

Phishing awareness

Most account compromises start with phishing. Legitimate Contoso emails will never ask you to enter your password into a linked page outside of account.contoso.internal or the standard Microsoft 365 sign-in page. Be especially cautious of urgent-sounding requests to "verify your account" or "reset your password immediately." Hover over links to check the real destination before clicking, and report suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook rather than simply deleting them.

Reporting a compromised account

If you suspect your account has been compromised — for example, you notice sign-in activity you don't recognize, or you accidentally entered your password on a suspicious site — change your password immediately from a trusted device, then contact the IT help desk as a Severity 1 issue so the security team can review account activity and revoke any active sessions. Do not wait for a scheduled password expiration to address a suspected compromise.
